logos.json holds SVG paths from Simple Icons (https://simple-icons.org, CC0-1.0). Canva and Photoshop
come from Simple Icons v11, since later releases removed them. The LinkedIn mark and the SEO icon were
added by hand. All brand logos are trademarks of their owners and are used only to identify the tools
and platforms named.

fonts.json holds glyph outlines from Bricolage Grotesque, Instrument Serif and JetBrains Mono, each
under the SIL Open Font License. The licence texts are the OFL-*.txt files in this folder.
